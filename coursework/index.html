Cloud Computing | HND Network Engineering, Year 2

Coursework

Two pieces of coursework run alongside the teaching days. Both are built in AWS, on the Thara Hospitals scenario, from the labs you do each afternoon.

Coursework Weight Individual or group Brief
Individual build portfolio 20% of the module Individual Portfolio brief
Group capstone 30% of the module Group of three (four where cohort size requires) Capstone brief

Dates and submissions are announced for your cohort.

Individual build portfolio

Six evidence packs, one after each of Days 1 to 6, each due before the next teaching day. The best five of your six count. Each pack shows what you built that day: a diagram, the key configuration, your prove it and break it records, your cost ledger rows, proof of teardown and a short reflection.

Read the portfolio brief. Templates: the evidence pack as a PDF form or as text, and the cost ledger.

Group capstone

Your group is the pilot team: you build components 4 to 12 of the Thara architecture in one member's account, inside the CFO's ceiling of USD 150 a month at production scale. You submit the running environment, a design justification, a cost estimate and a threat table after the review session, and defend the design at a group viva.

Read the capstone brief. It is available from the start of the module and is walked through in class on Day 4.
