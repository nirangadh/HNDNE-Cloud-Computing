# Individual build portfolio

**Weight:** 20% of the module. **Individual.**

## What you submit

Six evidence packs, one after each of Days 1 to 6. Each pack is due before the next teaching day; the pack for Day 6 is due before the review session. Dates are announced for your cohort. Use the [evidence pack template](../templates/evidence-pack.md). The best five of your six packs count, so one bad week does not sink you. A pack not submitted scores zero for that day.

Each pack contains: a diagram of what you built that day, the key configuration, the **prove it** output, your **break it** record, your cost ledger rows, proof of teardown, and a reflection of no more than 150 words. The lab sheet for each day lists exactly which screenshots and outputs to include.

## How each pack is marked

Five criteria, each worth 20 marks.

| Criterion | 0 to 7 | 8 to 13 | 14 to 20 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Completeness | Required evidence missing | All evidence present | All present and clearly labelled against the lab sheet's list |
| Technical correctness | Build does not match the lab sheet, or prove it fails or is missing | Build matches and prove it passes | Build matches, prove it passes, and break it is diagnosed with the right evidence |
| Security hygiene | Root used after Day 1, something public without reason, or keys left in place | No hygiene faults visible | No faults, and you explain one security choice you made beyond the sheet |
| Cost discipline | Something left running that should have been deleted, or ledger missing or inconsistent | Ledger complete and consistent with Cost Explorer | Ledger complete, consistent, and the day's cost explained against the estimate |
| Reflection | Missing or generic | Names one thing that surprised you | Names it, explains why it happened, and says what you would do differently |

Three things lose the relevant criterion regardless of how good the write-up is: signing in as root after Day 1; a NAT gateway or load balancer still running at the end of a lab day; a public bucket without a stated reason.

Late packs follow the standard late-submission rules.
