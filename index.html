Cloud Computing

Higher National Diploma in Network Engineering, Year 2

Seven teaching days on AWS, plus a review session.

Scan to open this site on your phone

Over seven teaching days you build, secure, monitor and cost a small cloud environment on Amazon Web Services, in your own AWS account, one piece at a time. Every lab adds a numbered component to the same architecture: a patient portal pilot for Thara Hospitals, a fictional private hospital group in Sri Lanka.
