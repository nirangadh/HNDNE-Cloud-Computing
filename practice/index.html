Practice questions

One practice question is posted after each of Days 1 to 6. Each is set in the Thara Hospitals scenario and has three parts: explain an idea, apply it to the scenario, then weigh a choice or a risk. Each question comes with guidance on what a strong answer covers.

Attempt each one without notes, in about 35 minutes, then compare with the guidance. Bring your attempts to the review session.
