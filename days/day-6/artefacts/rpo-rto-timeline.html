RPO and RTO on one line

Thara's patient records have two targets: lose at most one hour of data, and be back within two hours. Drag the sliders. The two on the left are decided before a failure, and they set the data lost. The two on the right are decided after it, and they set the time down. Watch which result moves.

The targets are Thara's: an RPO of one hour and an RTO of two hours for patient records. The picture is the same for any system; only the targets change. Thara Hospitals is fictional.
