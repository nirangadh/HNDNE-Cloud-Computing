Two hours on the clock

LO5LO6

This afternoon's lab, with you as the engineer on duty. You build the patient records database, delete it on purpose and bring it back. Thara allows two hours for that. Every wrong turn costs minutes, and the minutes are your recovery time.

How to play
  1. Read the situation and choose what to do. Every choice shows its cost in time or money, and its reason.
  2. There are several endings. Only one brings the records back inside the target, keeps the snapshot, and leaves nothing billing.
