What saves you?

LO3LO6

Thara's pilot now has five different kinds of protection, and each answers a different thing going wrong. Read what has happened, or what is needed, and choose the protection that answers it.

How to play
  1. Read the card and choose a bin. Keys 1 to 5 also work.
  2. Ask two questions of every card: is the data wrong, or is something not answering? And is a copy made before the trouble needed?
  3. You have four minutes for sixteen cards. Play without the timer if you would rather think than race. Then come four look-alike pairs.
