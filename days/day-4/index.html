THARA TODAY

A way out that is not a way in

On Day 3 you proved that Thara's web tier has no way out. Today it needs one: the servers must be patched, yet nothing on the internet may reach them. The laboratory reports must travel without touching the internet at all. A partner laboratory wants to send results in, and four branches are waiting their turn. Each of these is a route, and one of them bills by the hour.
