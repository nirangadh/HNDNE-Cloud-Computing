Which way out?

LO7

Thara's pilot has to connect to five different kinds of thing, and each has its own right path with its own price. Read what is needed and choose the path that serves it.

How to play
  1. Read the card and choose a bin. Keys 1 to 5 also work.
  2. Ask two questions of every card: what is at the other end, and who starts the conversation?
  3. You have four minutes for fifteen cards. Play without the timer if you would rather think than race. Then come four look-alike pairs.
