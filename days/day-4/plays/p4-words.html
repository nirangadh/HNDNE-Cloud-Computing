Words ladder: Day 4

Eleven words from today. Several are easy to confuse: a NAT gateway and an internet gateway, a gateway endpoint and a NAT gateway, peering and a Transit Gateway, a VPN and Direct Connect. For each one, climb three rungs.

How to play

  2. Keys 1 to 3 also work. The reason appears after every rung.
