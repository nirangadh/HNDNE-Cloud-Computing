Write the statement

LO5LO8

thara-web-role needs a policy, and a policy is made of statements. Each brief needs one statement: an effect, an action and a resource. Too wide is risky. A statement that matches nothing does not work.

How to play
  1. Read the brief, then choose a value for each of the three slots.
  2. Choose Check this rule. The verdict is valid, risky or wrong, with the reason.
  3. A brief scores when your first check is valid. You can keep trying until it is.
