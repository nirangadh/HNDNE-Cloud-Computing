Retire the key

LO5LO8

The Day 2 access key on thara-web-1 goes this afternoon, and a role takes its place. Here are seven steps of that change, shuffled. Done in the right order, the server is never left with too much access and never with too little.

How to play
  1. Put the steps in the order that works, first step at the top. Use the arrow buttons, or focus a step, press Space to lift it, the arrow keys to move it and Space to drop it.
  2. Choose Compare my order. You are scored against the order the Head of IT would use, which is the lab's order, one point for each step in the same place.
  3. One pair of steps could be done either way round. The reason the lab puts them as it does is given with the result.
