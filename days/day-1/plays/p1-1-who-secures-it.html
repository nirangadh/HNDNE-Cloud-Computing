The DPO's question, "who can read patient data?", has a different answer at each layer. Sort each job: always AWS's, always Thara's, or one that moves with the service.

How to play
  1. Read the card and choose a bin. Keys 1 to 3 also work.
  2. The third bin is for work that is Thara's on an EC2 server and AWS's on a managed database.
  3. You have three minutes for sixteen cards. Play without the timer if you would rather think than race. Then come two look-alike pairs.
