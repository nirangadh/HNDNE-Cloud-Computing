THARA TODAY

Three days on paper

During the last south-west monsoon, water entered the basement server room in Colombo. The patient portal and the laboratory reporting system were down for three days, and consultants at the branches worked from paper. The flood did not refund the servers. The CFO has approved USD 150 a month for a six-month pilot. Today you work out why the answer is not another server somewhere drier.
