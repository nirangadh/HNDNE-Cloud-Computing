THARA TODAY

A server you can build twice

The flood took Thara's two servers and the only copy of how they were built. Today the portal gets its first server in the cloud, small enough to cost about a cent an hour, and an image that can rebuild it in minutes in another zone. The laboratory reports get a bucket that no link can open by accident. And the CFO will ask why a stopped server is still on the bill.
