The point. Stop the instance and leave it for a while. Two meters stop. The volume meter does not: a stopped instance still has a disk, and the disk is still stored. Only Terminate ends every charge, and it also deletes the disk.
Rates are approximate figures for Asia Pacific (Mumbai) at the time of writing, rounded for clarity. The volume rate is an 8 GiB gp3 volume spread over a month. Check the AWS pricing pages before you rely on any number.
