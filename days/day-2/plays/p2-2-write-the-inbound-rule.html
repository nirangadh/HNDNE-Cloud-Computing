Write the inbound rule

LO5

thara-web-1 sits behind a security group, and this afternoon it needs four different things let in. Each brief needs one inbound rule. Too wide is risky, too narrow does not work.

How to play
  1. Read the brief, then choose a value for each of the three slots.
  2. Choose Check this rule. The verdict is valid, risky or wrong, with the reason.
  3. A brief scores when your first check is valid. You can keep trying until it is.
