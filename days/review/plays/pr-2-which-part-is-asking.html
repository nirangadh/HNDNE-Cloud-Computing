Which part is asking?

A question in parts asks three different things of you, and the verb is not always the clue. Read each task and say what kind of answer it wants: an explanation, something made for Thara, or a judgement.

How to play
  1. Read the card and choose a bin. Keys 1 to 3 also work.
  2. Ask: would the answer be the same for any organisation (explain)? Does it produce something for this one (apply)? Does it have to choose or weigh (evaluate)?
  3. You have three minutes for eighteen cards. Play without the timer if you would rather think than race. Then come four look-alike pairs.
